---
title: "Prerequisites"
description: "What you need before installing Dreeve: Docker with Docker Compose, and your activity files or a Strava account."
---

Dreeve is self-hosted: it runs on your own machine and your data stays there. To get started you need:

* [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/engine/install/) with [Docker Compose](https://docs.docker.com/compose/install/).
* Activity files (`.fit`, `.tcx` or `.gpx`) exported from your watch, bike computer or another platform.

That's it.

:::note
Strava is a fully supported activity source as well, all documented on the [Strava import](/importing/strava-import/) page.
:::
