title: "iGPSPORT Sync"
description: "Import your iGPSPORT activities into Dreeve with the community-maintained iGPSPORT Sync connector."
---

**[iGPSPORT Sync](https://github.com/julien-gasnier/dreeve-igpsport-sync)** is a community-maintained
connector that downloads your new [iGPSPORT](https://www.igpsport.com/) activities and writes them to
Dreeve's `watch/` folder.

```text
iGPSPORT → iGPSPORT Sync → watch/ → Dreeve imports it
```

The connector runs as a separate container alongside Dreeve and logs in with your iGPSPORT email and
password, or a JWT token. Dreeve imports the files through its existing
[file import pipeline](/importing/file-import/); no Dreeve API key is needed.

:::caution
The connector uses iGPSPORT's unofficial API, which may change without notice.
:::

:::note
This is a third-party project, maintained separately from Dreeve. See its
[repository and setup guide](https://github.com/julien-gasnier/dreeve-igpsport-sync#installation-step-by-step)
for current requirements and support.
:::
