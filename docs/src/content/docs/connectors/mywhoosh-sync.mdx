---
title: "MyWhoosh Sync"
description: "Import your MyWhoosh rides into Dreeve with the community-maintained MyWhoosh Sync connector."
---

**[MyWhoosh Sync](https://github.com/julien-gasnier/dreeve-mywhoosh-sync)** is a community-maintained
connector that downloads your new [MyWhoosh](https://www.mywhoosh.com/) activities and writes them to
Dreeve's `watch/` folder.

```text
MyWhoosh → MyWhoosh Sync → watch/ → Dreeve imports it
```

The connector runs as a separate container alongside Dreeve and logs in with your MyWhoosh email and
password. Dreeve imports the files through its existing [file import pipeline](/importing/file-import/);
no Dreeve API key is needed.

:::caution
The connector uses MyWhoosh's unofficial API, which may change without notice.
:::

:::note
This is a third-party project, maintained separately from Dreeve. See its
[repository and setup guide](https://github.com/julien-gasnier/dreeve-mywhoosh-sync#installation-step-by-step)
for current requirements and support.
:::
