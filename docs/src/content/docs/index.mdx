## Key Features

### Import & manage data

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Import activities">Use raw FIT/TCX/GPX files, sync from Garmin, Polar, Wahoo, Apple Health and more, or connect your Strava account</Card>
  <Card title="Admin panel">Manage activities, gear and every setting from your browser</Card>
  <Card title="Automation rules">Automatically fill in gear, commute flags, sport types and more</Card>
</CardGrid>

### Analysis

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Dashboard">See all your stats and charts at a glance, arranged the way you like</Card>
  <Card title="Activities">Browse a detailed list of everything you've done</Card>
  <Card title="Monthly view">Monthly stats with an interactive calendar</Card>
  <Card title="Eddington">Track the largest number E for which you covered at least E km on E different days</Card>
  <Card title="Segments & efforts">Dive into your segment history and times</Card>
  <Card title="Heatmap">Visualize where you’ve been active the most</Card>
</CardGrid>

### Gear

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Gear stats">Track how much you've used each bike, shoe, etc.</Card>
  <Card title="Maintenance tracking">Keep tabs on gear wear and tear</Card>
</CardGrid>

### Looking back

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Milestones">A timeline view of your key achievements and milestones over time</Card>
  <Card title="Rewind">A fun way to look back on your year in motion</Card>
  <Card title="Challenges">Browse the Strava challenges and trophies you have earned</Card>
  <Card title="Activity photos">Relive your moments with a photo archive</Card>
</CardGrid>

### Integrations & more

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="AI workout assistant">Get workout suggestions and insights from the AI provider of your choice, or a local Ollama model</Card>
  <Card title="MCP server">Connect Claude, Cursor or any other MCP client to your data</Card>
  <Card title="User badges">Shareable badges you can embed on your website, blog, or forum profiles</Card>
  <Card title="PWA support">Use it like a native app on your phone</Card>
</CardGrid>
